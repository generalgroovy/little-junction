A little help

Each cart carries a station symbol: ☀ Sun, ☾ Moon or ♧ Leaf. The three junction buttons change the outgoing tracks. A cart moves one track per step. Carts wait if the next junction is occupied.

Both upper and lower tracks can reach Moon. Match Sun using the upper branch and Leaf using the lower branch. You can turn several junctions before stepping. A wrong delivery pauses playback; Undo lets you try again.

Nothing moves until you choose Step time or Play slowly. Leaving the tab pauses playback. Start again returns to the same arrival order, and Undo can recover a restart.
